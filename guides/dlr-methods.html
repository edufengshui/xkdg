Da Liu Ren — the chart types (九宗門)

Reference · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

Every Da Liu Ren chart is cast the same way: the Month General is placed on the hour branch, the heaven plate turns with it, and the Four Readings (四課) are built from the day stem and the day branch. The Three Messages (三傳: Initial, Middle, Final) are then taken from the Four Readings with one of nine methods, checked in this order. The name of the method used is the yellow line under the Four Readings in the app; tap it to come to its paragraph here.

In all ordinary methods, once the Initial is found, the Middle is the heaven branch standing over the Initial's place on the earth plate, and the Final is the heaven branch standing over the Middle's place. The special methods (Pleiades, Separate Duty, Eight Specials, Hidden and Return Chant) have their own rules, given below.

1. Controlling methods 賊剋法

Look at the four readings, each made of an upper (heaven) branch over a lower one (the day stem's lodging or a branch). An upper controlling the lower is 克 (control); a lower controlling the upper is 賊 (rebellion, "thief"). A rebellion from below always takes precedence over a control from above.

元首 Controlling: Head of State (Yuan Shou)

When: exactly one reading has the upper controlling the lower, and no reading has the lower rebelling.

Initial: the upper branch of that reading.

Meaning: the clearest and most orderly chart: the superior acts on the inferior, matters move from above, in the proper order. Things start from the top and from the outside; it favours the first move, the senior, the host.

重審 Controlling: Re-examination (Chong Shen)

When: exactly one reading has the lower rebelling against the upper (下賊上).

Initial: the upper branch of that reading.

Meaning: the movement comes from below, against the order: the inferior challenges the superior. Matters must be examined twice, they start with resistance; it favours the second move, the guest, patience and careful review.

2. Comparing 比用 (Knowing One 知一)

When: two or more readings have a control of the same kind (several rebellions, or several controls with no rebellion).

Initial: among those, the upper branch whose yin / yang matches the day stem (yang day → yang branch, yin day → yin branch). If exactly one matches, it is the Initial.

Meaning: several possibilities, one is chosen by affinity: choose what is close and similar to you; the matter involves comparison, competition, a choice among peers.

3. Wading through Harm 涉害

When: Comparing cannot decide (none matches the day stem, or more than one does).

Initial: for each candidate, count the controls it "wades through" going from its earth position back to its own home position on the earth plate; the one that suffers the deepest harm (most controls) becomes the Initial. If still equal, the one standing on a 孟 (Yin, Shen, Si, Hai) is preferred, then on a 仲 (Zi, Wu, Mao, You).

Meaning: a difficult, long road with obstacles to cross; matters are slow and laborious, the deepest difficulty must be faced first.

4. Remote control 遙剋 — Arrow 蒿矢 / Shooting 彈射

When: no reading has any control between its upper and lower.

Look then at the relation between the upper branches of the 2nd, 3rd and 4th readings and the day stem:

  • Remote: Arrow 蒿矢 — an upper branch controls the day stem from a distance: that branch is the Initial.
  • Remote: Shooting 彈射 — when none controls the stem, the day stem controls an upper branch from a distance: that branch is the Initial.

With more than one candidate, choose by yin / yang affinity as in Comparing.

Meaning: the influence comes from far away, indirectly; power is weak like an arrow of grass or a pellet shot from a distance. News, events at a distance, things that touch you without direct contact.

5. Pleiades 昴星

When: no control in the readings and no remote control either.

Initial: on a yang day, look up: the heaven branch standing over the earth 酉 (You). On a yin day, look down: the earth branch lying under the heaven 酉.

Middle and Final: yang day — Middle = the upper of the day branch's reading, Final = the upper of the day stem's reading; yin day — the other way round.

Meaning: 酉 is the gate of the West, of autumn and of punishment: a hidden, uneasy situation, things not yet visible, concealment, waiting; often linked with fear, illness or legal worry in the classics.

6. Separate Duty 別責

When: the Four Readings contain only three different readings (one is repeated), and there is no control or remote control.

Initial: on a yang day, the heaven branch over the lodging of the stem that combines with the day stem (干合); on a yin day, the branch of the day branch's three-harmony group that comes before it (支前三合). Middle and Final are both the upper of the day stem's reading.

Meaning: something incomplete, a missing piece; one has to rely on others, on a partner or an outside party; matters are entrusted to someone else.

7. Eight Specials 八專

When: the day stem lodges in the same palace as the day branch (甲寅, 庚申, 丁未, 己未), so the readings come in two identical pairs, and there is no control.

Initial: on a yang day, count three branches forward from the upper of the 1st reading; on a yin day, count three branches backward from the upper of the 4th reading. Middle and Final are both the upper of the day stem's reading.

Meaning: concentration, everything in one place; inside and outside are not separated — family, intimate or private matters, people living together; also confusion of roles.

8. Hidden Chant 伏吟 (Fu Yin)

When: the Month General falls on its own branch at that hour, so every heaven branch sits on the same earth branch: the plates do not move.

Initial: if a reading has a control, use it; otherwise on a yang day the upper of the day stem's reading, on a yin day the upper of the day branch's reading. Middle and Final follow the punishments (刑) of the branch.

Meaning: stillness, things hidden or stuck, waiting, nothing moves yet; worry kept inside, a situation that repeats itself.

9. Return Chant 返吟 (Fan Yin)

When: every heaven branch stands on its opposite earth branch (six clashes 六沖).

Initial: if the readings have a control, the Controlling methods apply (the app then writes e.g. "Return Chant · Head of State"); without control, the Initial is the Post Horse (驛馬) of the day branch.

Meaning: reversal, coming and going, things turned upside down; movement, travel, separation and return, a decision that is changed.

This page summarises the classical rules so the yellow line of the chart can be read. Interpretation always depends on the whole chart: the generals, the relations to the day stem, voids, the season and the question asked.
