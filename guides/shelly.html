XKDG App — The Aquarium Plug (Shelly)

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

A Shelly smart plug lets the XKDG app switch the aquarium light on by itself at the favourable hours. You connect the plug to a saved house once; after that the assistant computes the plan and the XKDG server switches the plug at the right times, even when your phone is off. This guide covers:

  1. What you need.
  2. Three values from the Shelly app: server, key and Device ID.
  3. Connecting the plug in the XKDG app and testing it.
  4. Programming the light with the assistant.
  5. Problems, disconnecting, privacy.

Part 1 — What you need

  • A Shelly plug (for example Shelly Plus Plug S), added to your own account in the Shelly app and connected to the Shelly cloud (in the app the plug shows as online).
  • The aquarium light on that plug. Only the light: pump, filter and heater stay on a normal socket, always on.
  • The house saved in Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES, with its aquarium (direction) and its chart, so the water star is known.
  • Your XKDG licence active on this phone.

Part 2 — Three values from the Shelly app

1. Server and key

  1. Open the Shelly app and tap the profile icon (User settings).
  2. Tap Authorization cloud key, then Get key.
  3. Copy the key (a long string of letters and digits).
  4. Note the server shown on the same page, for example https://shelly-123-eu.shelly.cloud. In XKDG you can paste it with or without https://.
The key opens your Shelly account. Paste it only into the XKDG app, never in a message or an email. If you ever think it is known to others, generate a new one in the Shelly app and connect the plug again.

2. Device ID

  1. In the Shelly app open the plug of the aquarium light.
  2. Tap Settings → Device information.
  3. Copy the Device ID: usually 12 letters and digits (6 on older plugs), for example a0b765c3d2e4.
The menus of the Shelly app can change slightly between versions. The names to look for are always Authorization cloud key and Device ID.

Part 3 — Connect the plug in XKDG

  1. Go to Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES.
  2. On the house card, tap the ☰ at the top right, then 🔌 Aquarium plug (Shelly).
  3. A window opens: 🔌 Aquarium plug (Shelly) — house name.
  4. Name: leave Aquarium or write your own.
  5. Paste the Server, the Authorization cloud key and the Device ID.
  6. Check Longitude of the house and UTC (winter). They give the True Solar Time of the house, on which the hours are computed. The longitude is filled from your GPS: if you are not at the house now, type the longitude of the house (east positive: Rome 12.5, Vienna 16.4; west negative: New York −74.0). UTC is the winter offset (Italy and Austria +1); summer time is added by the app.
  7. Tap 🔌 Connect. The XKDG server checks your licence and reads the plug.
  8. When it answers ✅ Connected, tap 💡 Test ON and ⏻ Test OFF: the light must switch on and off. Status tells you whether the plug is ON or OFF now.
Each house has its own plug. A licence can connect up to 5 plugs. Connecting the same house again replaces its old plug.
Do not rename the house after connecting: the plug is linked to the house name. If you rename it, open 🔌 Aquarium plug again and connect it again.

Part 4 — Program the light with the assistant

  1. Open the 💬 assistant and write, for example: "program the aquarium of Casa Roma" (your house name).
  2. The assistant computes the plan and shows it first: each day the hour the light switches on, how long it stays on, and why.
  3. Nothing reaches the plug yet. Tap Procedi to send the plan, or Annulla.
  4. The plan runs until the start of the next solar month. Then ask the assistant to program it again.
Other questions you can ask:
  • "Is the aquarium of Casa Roma on now?" · "Switch it off."
  • "What is scheduled for the aquarium of Casa Roma?": the plan really stored on the server.
  • "Pause the aquarium plan" / "Resume it".

If a switch-on or switch-off failed (plug offline, Wi-Fi down), the server keeps trying for 20 minutes. If it still fails, the assistant tells you at the start of its next answer, with date and hour.

Disconnect

☰ of the house → 🔌 Aquarium plug (Shelly) → 🗑 Disconnect the plug. The plug, its key and its plan are deleted from the XKDG server; the light no longer switches by itself.

Privacy

  • The XKDG server keeps your Shelly server, key and Device ID only to switch your plug.
  • Your phone receives a token that opens only your plug. Nobody else's plug can be reached with it.
  • To withdraw access completely, disconnect the plug and generate a new key in the Shelly app.
