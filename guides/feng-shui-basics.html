XKDG App — Feng Shui Basics

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

This guide covers the everyday work in the 🧭 FENG SHUI mode of the XKDG app:

  1. House Profiles: save a house with its facing, period, floors, people and doors.
  2. The luopan and the flying stars: how to read the wheel and how to enter a chart by hand.
  3. Date scans: the 🔎 SCAN of a door, and the Water, Bed and Desk sections.
  4. Floor plan: put the 8 palaces and the flying stars on a picture of the plan.

📷 Photo sectors and 📏 Plan builder have their own guide. The CHARTS tab (where the flat flying star chart now lives) also has its own guide.

Before you start

  • Load a person in ☯ PERSON A (name, birth date and time). House Profiles always follow the person who is loaded. With nobody loaded the app says "Load a person (A or B) to manage house profiles."
  • Set FROM (start date) and DAYS in the toolbar. Every date scan in this guide uses this range.
  • Tap 🧭 FENG SHUI.
Rules the app follows. A door facing must sit on a 正神 Zheng Shen hexagram, and the water on a 零神 Ling Shen hexagram. The water must be within ±70° of the facing. For the facing ↔ water pair, "adding" only counts when the two numbers add up to exactly 10. People are checked first by the year pillar, then by the day pillar. All hours are in local true solar time.

Part 1 — House Profiles

1. Create a house

  1. In the green box 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES, tap + Add a new house.
  2. Type the house name (for example "Vienna apartment").
  3. If the luopan already holds a facing and period, the app asks "Import the current Main Luopan consultation…?". OK uses those values. Cancel starts an empty house.
  4. The app asks "Who is this house registered to? (owner — occupant #1)". Type the owner's name. If the owner is not Person A, it also asks the birth date (DD-MM-YYYY) and birth time (HH:MM, leave blank if unknown).
  5. The new house appears as a card and becomes the active house (green dot ●).
You can also fill House Facing (°) and Period (1-9) in the ⭐ FLYING STARS box and tap 💾 SAVE. The app asks which house to save into (0 = New house) and, if there are several floors, which floor.

2. The house card

  • ● marks the active house. Tap ○ on another card to make it active.
  • The card shows Facing … · Period … next to the name.
  • ▸ Open shows the details and tools. ▾ Hide folds them again.
  • Top right: 📂 Load (puts the house on the luopan), ✏ Rename, 🗑 House (delete), 📁 Archive (link to the client's Google Drive folder).
  • Inside: ✏ Address and, once an address is saved, 🗺 Map. To measure the facing you can use 📍 Measure facing (satellite map) or ☀️ Sun facing (shadow on a card at a window, no compass).

3. Facing, period and floors

  1. Open the card. Next to 🏢 Floor: you see the facing and period, for example Facing 175° · Period 9 ✎. Tap that text to change them. If nothing is set it says "no facing/period" in orange.
  2. To add a floor, tap + Add floor and type its name (for example "Attic").
  3. The app asks "Same facing & period as the rest of the house for this floor?"
    • OK = same facing for all floors. Changing the facing later changes it for the whole house.
    • Cancel = this floor has its own facing and period; the app asks for them.
  4. Use the Floor drop-down to switch floors. Doors, waters, settings and the floor plan belong to the floor you select. ✏ Rename renames the floor, 🗑 deletes it (only when there is more than one).

4. People: owner, guest and extra members

  • The owner (occupant #1) is loaded into Person A whenever you load the house.
  • 👥 Add a new guest adds occupant #2 (name, birth date, birth time). The guest goes into Person B, so every scan considers both people. A drop-down lets you choose:
    • Cohabit (owner + guest) — both are scanned;
    • Guest as #1 (owner away) — the owner is hidden from the scans (kept, not deleted).
    🗑 Remove guest removes the guest.
  • ➕ Extra members (year only): pick saved people from ➕ Add member…. Only their birth year enters the date/hour scans, and everybody must connect. Six people maximum. Tap × on a name to remove it.

5. Doors (external and internal)

  1. In the 🚪 XKDG row of the card, tap + External Door (front door, façade) or + Internal Door (a room door).
  2. Type a name (for example "Study door").
  3. Type the XKDG Facing degree of the door.
  4. Type the Water degree (for an internal door it is called Fountain). Leave it empty if there is none.
  5. External doors only — the app asks the palace where the door IS, seen from the centre of the house (not where it faces): N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW (or Kan, Gen…). This is optional: leave it empty if you do not know it. QMDJ Li Qi uses it as the Qi mouth.
  6. The door appears under 🚪 XKDG Doors with a badge EXTERNAL or INTERNAL, for example "Facing: 172° · Water: 140° · in Li S".
  7. ✏ edits the door (name, facing, water, palace). ✕ removes it.
If you type a palace the app does not recognise, it says "Palace not recognised - left empty". Edit the door with ✏ to set it.

6. Backup

At the top of House Profiles, ⬇ Backup downloads a .json file with all houses, people and settings. ⬆ Restore loads it back. Make a backup before changing phone.

Part 2 — The luopan and the flying stars

1. Show the flying stars

  1. Load the house (📂 Load), or type House Facing (°) and Period (1-9) in the ⭐ FLYING STARS box.
  2. The stars appear on the luopan. If not, tap ⭐ Show Stars (it then reads ⭐ Hide Stars).
  3. Under the wheel a line shows the period, the mountains and the centre stars, for example 第9運 · …山…向 | Center: ….
替卦 Off switches the chart to its 替卦 Replacement version (near a mountain boundary). It is off by default: it is your own judgment call. When on, the line under the wheel shows 替卦 Repl.

2. Read the wheel

  • The 64 hexagrams are tinted: red = 正神 Zheng Shen, blue = 零神 Ling Shen.
  • The PERIOD button (NOW → 2044 / POST 2044) sets which numbers count as Zheng Shen: 6-9 now, 1-4 after 2044.
  • The buttons above the wheel choose what it shows: ⭐ FS only, 🚪 XKDG only, ⭐🚪 Both. 🏠 Floorplan shows the saved floor plan instead of the wheel (see Part 4).
  • When the XKDG layer is drawn, a legend appears under the wheel: ✓ Door Facing for date, Water for facing, Selected facing, Water ±70° zone, Invalid water.

3. The ☰ menu and the ⤢ button

  • ☰ (top right of the wheel) → ⤴ Facing up turns the luopan so the house facing is at the top. Choose ⤺ South up to turn it back. Use Facing up when you hold the phone looking out of the front door.
  • ⤢ (bottom right) switches to the full-size luopan; ⤡ goes back to compact. Only the size changes; the phone remembers your choice.

4. Enter a chart by hand (⭐ Manual)

  1. In the ⭐ FLYING STARS box tap ⭐ Manual.
  2. A 3×3 grid opens, South at top. Each palace has three boxes: 山 Sitting (green), 向 Facing (red), 運 Base (black). It starts filled with the normal chart.
  3. Change the numbers (1–9) you need and tap Apply. Cancel closes without changes.
  4. The badge ⭐ Manual ON appears. The manual chart is saved with the active house and floor and comes back when you load the house.
  5. ↺ Reset to auto returns to the normal chart.
With the assistant: inside the Manual grid, 🤖 AI chart starts a guided interview: base chart, which water stars you require in which palaces, which to exclude. The assistant finds the chart and fills the grid — it never applies it. You check it and tap Apply yourself.

Part 3 — Date scans

1. 🔎 SCAN of a door

Every saved door with a facing has its own 🔎 SCAN button. It looks for dates that match the door's Zheng Shen facing, its Ling Shen water and the people of the house.

  1. Check that FROM is set in the toolbar.
  2. Tap 🔎 SCAN on the door line. The results open right under the door.
  3. The app first checks the door. It stops with a message if:
    • the facing is NOT Zheng Shen;
    • the water is NOT Ling Shen;
    • the water is more than ±70° from the facing;
    • the water hexagram does not communicate with the facing hexagram.
    In each case edit the door (✏) and try again. A door without water is scanned on the facing alone.

2. How to read the results

  • The title says "Matching dates for this setup: N found", and below it the facing and water hexagrams and the Pure YY star.
  • Sort: BEST (highest score first) or LIST (by date). Range: 1m 3m 6m 1y 2y 5y, and ✕ Reset to go back to the toolbar DAYS.
  • ☆ QIMEN ☆ OFF turns on the Qimen check: dates with a Qimen match move to the top with a ☆, and the ☆ Qimen column shows the palace card (tap it for the full chart).
  • Columns: Date · Hour (best hour pillar, with its clock window) · XKDG Relations (red line = date ↔ facing, blue line = date ↔ water) · Pure YY Star (✓ good, ✗ bad) · Score.
  • Rows in yellow with 🔗 Full BL are Family Blood Link dates: they always go to the top in BEST.
  • Tap a row to open that date in the main calendar.
  • If Person A or B is missing, an orange box offers + Add Person A / + Add Person B: birth data adds the personal stars and improves the ranking.

3. The Water section

  1. On the house card tap 💧 Add Water (in the ⭐ Flying Stars row). The section 🌊 Setting up the Water opens; ← Close goes back.
  2. ① General water feature (aquarium / fountain) — by palace: choose the Palace and the Days, then 🔎 SCAN dates. You get a list of Date · Hour · Score (Qimen, plus XKDG when a person is loaded). 📊 QMDJ shows the chart of that hour. 💾 Save to house stores the water feature in the house.
  3. Tick 🧭 + Zheng Shen / Ling Shen (XKDG) to also check the house's main door facing and water.
  4. ② 🚪 Internal Door Facing: type the Door Facing (°) · 正神 ZS, or use 📍 Measure facing. If the facing is not Zheng Shen, the app offers the nearest one (OK = use it).
  5. Type the Water (°) · 零神 LS, or tap 💡 Suggest: the app proposes the closest good Ling Shen water within ±70° with a good Pure YY star. Tap Apply to use it.
  6. Under the wheel you see the door and water hexagrams, the distance (±70° ✓) and the Pure YY star. Tap 🔎 Find matching dates: the results read exactly like the door SCAN above.

4. The Bed section

  1. On the house card tap 🛏 Add Bed.
  2. Choose the Bed palace (where the bed is). The app shows the three stars of that palace, with the Mountain ★ star highlighted.
  3. Type the Bed Sitting (°) — headboard. The sitting hexagram must be 正神 Zheng Shen ✓. If not, the app shows the Nearest Zheng Shen sitting with a Use button.
  4. Communication with the sitting hexagram: for each person (A, B) it says communicates (and how) or no communication, and suggests a sitting that suits everybody.
  5. 💧 Fountain position: measured from the bed facing (sitting + 180°), in front of the bed, never behind the headboard. The app gives the Ideal position and other valid ones, each with Use. Valid positions are blue on the luopan. The fountain field is optional.
  6. Tap 🔎 SCAN lucky dates to move the bed.

5. The Desk section

  1. On the house card tap 🪑 Add Desk.
  2. If A and B are both loaded, choose the Person who sits.
  3. Choose the Desk palace and type the Desk Facing (°). It must be Zheng Shen and communicate with the person; otherwise use the suggested facing (Use).
  4. The app lists the Propitious water positions (Ling Shen, within ±70°, matching the facing and the person). The 💧 Fountain (°) field is optional.
  5. Tap 🔎 SCAN lucky dates to set up the desk & water.

6. How Bed and Desk results read

A date is lucky when the loop closes: ① the date communicates with the bed sitting (or desk facing), and for every person ② the person communicates with the placement and ③ the person communicates with the date. Each row shows the date (dd/mm/yyyy), its pillar, 運 and 氣, and the kind of link for every pair. Tap a row to open the date in the main calendar. If nothing is found, try a wider range or another sitting/facing.

7. Save the setting in the house

  1. At the bottom of each section, in the box 💾 … — settings, check Save in: 🏠 house and 🏢 floor.
  2. Tap 💾 Save current and give it a name (for example "Aquarium SE").
  3. On the house card the line Saved: now shows it (🛏 Bed, 🪑 Desk in the XKDG row; 💧 Water in the Flying Stars row). Tap its name to show it on the luopan and to ✓ Save a new name, 🔎 Scan dates, ✏️ Open to edit or 🗑 Remove.
Inside a section the flying stars are not repeated on the wheel. Tap ⭐ Recall Flying Stars to show them again (⭐ Hide Flying Stars hides them).

Part 4 — Floor plan with the 8 palaces

1. Open the tool from the house

  1. Open the house card, choose the right Floor, and tap 📐 Import a floorplan.
  2. The window 🏠 Floor Plan Flying Stars — house name opens, with the house facing and period already filled.
Open it only from the house card. Opened anywhere else it says "Read-only — you cannot save here" and there is no 💾 Save to house button.

2. Load the plan and find the centre

  1. Tap 📷 Camera (take a photo now) or 🖼️ Gallery / Files (an existing image, Google Drive too).
  2. With Rectangles (drag to cover area) selected: drag, or tap two opposite corners, to draw rectangles that cover the plan. An L-shaped house needs several rectangles.
  3. Tap Find center. Undo removes the last rectangle, Clear removes all.
  4. Or choose Manual center (tap a point) and tap the centre yourself.
If the app says "The center falls in a gap between rectangles" or "…outside the covered area (irregular plan)", check the missing-sector situation.

3. Draw the 8 palaces and save

  1. Check Facing ° and Period.
  2. In Facing is on the image: tap the side of the picture where the house faces: Top, Right, Bottom or Left.
  3. Tap Draw chart. Each palace shows the mountain star (山, green), the facing star (向, red) and the base star (運, small). If the house has a manual chart, the plan uses it ("Using the saved MANUAL chart").
  4. Tap 💾 Save to house. The app says "Floor plan saved to this house ✓".
  5. 📥 Save image and 🖨 Print give you a picture or a printout.

4. See the plan again

  • On the house card: 🖼 Floor plan shows it large, in place of the luopan. ✏ opens it again to edit. 🗑 Plan removes it.
  • Above the wheel, 🏠 Floorplan does the same for the active floor. ↩ Luopan goes back.
Very large photos are reduced automatically to fit the phone's storage. If it says "Storage is full", remove an unused house or another floor plan.
