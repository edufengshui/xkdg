Missing communication and remedies

Person analysis · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

1. What the app checks

Each of the four birth pillars (Hour, Day, Month, Year) is a hexagram with two numbers: the Element (red, above) and the Period (blue, below). Two pillars communicate in a dimension when their numbers form a Hetu pair (1-6, 2-7, 3-8, 4-9), an Adding (sum 5, 10 or 15) or are the same number.

A pillar is isolated in a dimension when it communicates with none of the other three. The check is made separately for Elements and Periods.

A chart in a full Blood Link (all four pillars in one family), or a Two-Family Bond of two families that share no child, already communicates completely: no pillar is isolated and no remedy is needed.

3. The remedy

The remedy is a hexagram that does two things at the same time:

  1. in the dimension with the gap, it links the isolated pillar, speaking the language the other pillars of that dimension already speak (Adding 5/10/15 or Hetu);
  2. in the other dimension, it keeps the connection with the pillars, in their same language.

In the example the Periods speak Adding (2+3 = 5), so the remedy's Period must add up with the isolated 4: Period 1 (4+1 = 5) or 6 (4+6 = 10). The Elements speak Adding too (4+6, 1+4), so its Element must add up with one of them — for instance Element 6 (6+4 = 10). A hexagram of Element 6 and Period 1 is a remedy.

★ The family alternative

When the isolated pillar shares a family with another pillar of the chart, that family already links it. A hexagram of that family does not need to link the isolated dimension: it only has to keep the connection in the other dimension. Only a family present in the chart counts.

A chart can also have no gap at all: a full Blood Link, or a Two-Family Bond of two families that share no child, already communicates completely.

4. Two ways to apply it

  1. A date to choose — a favourable date (at least one hour with an XKDG relation) whose Day or Year pillar is a remedy hexagram.
  2. A Feng Shui hexagram to activate — the luopan sector (in degrees, ZS or LS) of a remedy hexagram, to activate in the house.

5. Where to find it in the app

  • Person panel → the box 🔧 Missing communication under the XKDG relations (tap it to open).
  • 👤 PERSON tab (next to TABLES): every favourable date with the remedy in the FROM / DAYS range, with its favourable hours (tap one to open it in the main chart), and every direction to activate: the remedies first, then the ★ family alternative.
  • Assistant: ask "where is the missing communication in my chart and what are my remedies?".
