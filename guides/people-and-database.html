XKDG App — People & the Person Database

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

Almost everything in the XKDG app works for a person: date selection, Qi Men, Feng Shui of a house. This guide shows you how to:

  1. Enter a person in Person A or Person B and see their Bazi.
  2. Save the person, and load them again later from the Person Database.
  3. Keep your people in order with folders (and subfolders).
  4. Add people to a house (guest #2 and extra members).
  5. Back up everything to your own Google Drive or Dropbox and use it on a second device.

Before you start

  • Update the app: close it completely and open it again.
  • Have the person's birth date, clock time of birth and birth city ready. If the time is not known, you can still work with fewer pillars (see Part 1).
  • Your saved people are kept on this device (in this browser). To have them on a second device, or as a backup, use ☁ Drive Sync (Part 6) or 📦 Dropbox Sync (Part 7).
All times are true solar time. You type the clock time from the birth certificate. The app converts it to the local true solar time of the birth place, using its longitude and time zone. The day pillar changes at true-solar midnight, not at clock midnight. This is why the birth place matters.

Part 1 — Enter a person

On the main page there are two person panels: ☯ PERSON A (green) and ☯ PERSON B (purple). They work the same way. Use A for the main person; use B for a second person (a partner, a client's child, a business partner…).

1. Set the birth place (top of the page)

The birth place is read from the location row at the top: UTC, LONG (longitude) and the 📍 City… box.

  1. Tap the 📍 City… box and start typing the birth city. Choose it from the list.
  2. Check that LONG and UTC have changed to that city.
  3. If the city is not in the list, type its longitude in LONG and its standard time zone in UTC yourself (e.g. 1 for Italy, 5.5 for India).
The time zone of a city can change over the years. The city box uses the rules in force on the date in the top date field. For an old birth date, first put the birth date in the top date field, then choose the city.
Do not use GPS for a birth chart, unless the person was born where you are now. GPS sets your position.

2. Fill in the person panel

  1. NAME: type the name. It also appears next to "☯ PERSON A" in the panel header.
  2. PILLARS: choose how much you know:
    • 4 — Year·Month·Day·Hour: date and time are known (normal case).
    • 3 — Year·Month·Day: the date is known, the time is not.
    • 2 — Year·Month: only year and month count.
    • 1 — Year only: you only know the year pillar (see step 3).
  3. BIRTH DATE: choose the date (shown for 2, 3 and 4 pillars).
  4. HOUR: type the clock time of birth (shown only with 4 pillars).
  5. DST: if summer time was in force on that day in the birth place, tap the panel's own DST: OFF button so it reads DST: ON. (Shown only with 4 pillars.)
  6. Tap CALCULATE.

3. A "year only" person (Jia Zi year)

Sometimes you only know the person's year pillar, for example a family member of a client.

  1. In PILLARS choose 1 — Year only. The date and hour boxes disappear.
  2. In YEAR PILLAR pick the Jia Zi year from the list of 60 (e.g. 甲子 Jiǎ Zǐ, 丙午 Bǐng Wǔ).
  3. Tap CALCULATE. Only the YEAR ★ column is shown in full; the others are faded.

4. What you see: the person's Bazi

  • Four columns: HOUR, DAY, MONTH, YEAR ★. Each shows the stem, the branch, the Nayin and the XKDG hexagram of that pillar.
  • Pillars you did not give (fewer than 4) are shown faded.
  • Below, ⬡ XKDG RELATIONS lists how the person's pillars relate to the date at the top of the page. ☆ Stars OFF adds or hides the stars in that list.
  • ▲ Hide Pillars / ▼ Show Pillars hides or shows the stem, branch and Nayin cells.
  • The first time you calculate a name, all the details are open. Next times they stay closed: tap ▸ Show 4P + XKDG details to open them (and ▾ Hide 4P + XKDG details to close).
  • With both A and B calculated, ♡ COMPAT (in Person B) checks the compatibility between them.

5. Switch a person off

Tap the header ☯ PERSON A (or B) to hide that person. They are then left out of the scans, and the arrow shows ▸ Show. Tap the header again to bring them back with the same data. After switching, tap SCAN again to refresh the results.

When you open the app again, it brings back the last person you used in A and in B.

Part 2 — Save a person

  1. Enter the person as in Part 1, with the birth city in the top row.
  2. Tap SAVE in the person panel.
  3. The name now appears as a chip in the row under the panel, next to 📋 DB.

When you save, the app stores the name, date, time, number of pillars, the Jia Zi year (if "year only"), and the birth place (longitude, UTC and the panel's DST). From then on the person's chart is always calculated for the birth place, even when the top row shows your own location again.

Re-saving overwrites the birth place. SAVE always takes the place that is in the top row at that moment. If you change a saved person (e.g. you correct the hour), first choose their birth city again in the top row, then tap SAVE.
The 💾 SAVE in the top row is different: it saves your own longitude, UTC and DST as the default for the next time you open the app. It does not save a person.

Part 3 — Load a saved person

1. From the row under the panel (quick)

  • Tap a name chip: the person is loaded into that panel and calculated.
  • The × next to a chip only hides it from this row. The person stays in the database.
  • The 📁 All menu at the start of the row shows only one folder (Part 4). Row A and row B each have their own menu.

2. From the Person Database (all people)

  1. Tap 📋 DB (in the row under Person A or Person B). The 📋 Person Database opens.
  2. Sort with A→Z or Recent (last saved first).
  3. Each card shows: a ★, a small A or B badge (the panel where the person was saved), the name and the birth date.
  4. Tap → A to load the person into Person A, or → B to load them into Person B. The window closes and the chart is calculated.
  5. Close the window with ✕ (top right) or by tapping outside it.

Other things you can do on a card:

  • ★: pin the person to the top of the list (tap again to unpin).
  • Note about this person…: type a short note; it is kept with the person.
  • hidden + Show: the person was hidden from the quick row; Show puts them back.
  • The 📁 menu: the person's folder (Part 4).
  • × (red): deletes the person permanently, after a confirmation.
For a year-only person, load them with → A / → B from the database: this also restores the "1 — Year only" setting and the Jia Zi year.
When you load a person, the app also loads their house profile in Feng Shui, if they have one.

Part 4 — Folders

Folders keep your people in order: Mine, Students, Clients, Friends are ready to use. A folder can have subfolders: write the path with /, for example Clients/Rossi or Clients/2026/Rossi. A person's houses always follow the person's folder.

1. Put a person in a folder

  1. Open 📋 DB and find the person's card.
  2. Open the 📁 menu on the card (it shows 📁 — no folder — if the person has none).
  3. Choose a folder. Or choose ➕ New folder… and type a name (use / for subfolders). If the person is already in a folder, you can also choose ➕ New subfolder in "…".

The same 📁 menu is also on each house card in 🧭 FENG SHUI → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES.

2. Show only one folder

Every list has its own folder menu: the row under Person A, the row under Person B, the Folder: menu at the top of the database, and the house members picker. Choose:

  • 📁 All: everybody;
  • a folder: the people in that folder and its subfolders;
  • — Unfiled: people without a folder.

The number in brackets is how many people are inside. Each list remembers its choice on this device, so you can, for example, show Clients in row A and Mine in row B.

3. Manage folders (new, rename / move, delete)

  1. In the database, tap ⚙ Manage folders. The 📁 Folders box opens with all folders as a tree.
  2. ➕ New folder: a new top-level folder (you can write a path with /).
  3. ➕ Sub on a folder's line: a new subfolder inside it.
  4. ✏️ Rename: write the full new path. This renames the folder, or moves it: e.g. change Rossi into Clients/2026/Rossi. Subfolders and people move with it. If the new path already exists, the app asks whether to merge the two folders.
  5. 🗑: deletes the folder and its subfolders. People are never deleted: they move up to the parent folder (or to Unfiled). The app tells you how many people will move and asks for confirmation.
  6. Tap ⚙ Manage folders again to close the box.

Part 5 — People in a house

In 🧭 FENG SHUI → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES, every house belongs to its owner (the person in Person A). You can add more people who live there.

1. Guest #2 (counted like Person B)

  1. On the house card, tap 👥 Add a new guest.
  2. Type the name, the birth date (DD-MM-YYYY) and the birth time (HH:MM, leave blank if unknown).
  3. The card shows 👥 Guest #2. The guest is also added to the Person Database.
  4. Choose Cohabit (owner + guest) to scan both, or Guest as #1 (owner away) to leave the owner out of the scans. 🗑 Remove guest removes the guest from the house.

2. Extra members (year only)

  1. Open the house card with ▸ Open.
  2. In the line ➕ Extra members (year only), open the menu ➕ Add member… and choose a saved person. Use the folder menu next to it to find them faster.
  3. The person appears as a chip. Tap its × to remove them from the house.
  • Only the birth year of extra members enters the date and hour scans (aquarium activation and the other operations).
  • The owner, guest #2 and every extra member must all connect with the day.
  • Six people maximum per house (owner + guest #2 + extra members). When the house is full the card says 6 people max.
Only saved people appear in the ➕ Add member… list. Save them first (Part 2).

Part 6 — Backup and a second device (☁ Drive Sync)

Drive Sync saves all your app data (people, folders, houses, settings) as one file, xkdg-sync.json, in your own Google Drive. The app only sees that one file.

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the app and tap ☁ Drive Sync (just above the version number).
  2. The first time, the field ⚙ Google OAuth Client ID must be filled in once. Without it the app says "First paste your Google Client ID".
  3. Tap ⬆ Save to Drive to save this device's data. Google asks you to sign in and allow access. The status line shows "✓ Saved to Drive" and the time.
  4. On the other device, open the same panel and tap ⬇ Load from Drive. Confirm: this replaces that device's data with the Drive copy, then the app reloads.
Last save wins. Always Load on a device before you start working on it, and Save when you finish. If the Drive copy is newer than this device's last sync, the app warns you before overwriting it: choose Cancel and Load first if you want to keep those changes.
Auto-sync (this device): tick it and the app checks Drive for a newer copy when you open it (it asks before loading) and saves your changes a few seconds after you edit. Conflicts are never overwritten automatically. The line "Last sync on this device…" shows when this device last synced.

Part 7 — Backup and a second device with Dropbox (📦 Dropbox Sync)

Dropbox Sync does the same job as Drive Sync, with your own Dropbox. It saves all your app data (people, folders, houses, settings) as one file, xkdg-sync.json, in the folder Apps › XKDG of your Dropbox. The app only sees that folder, nothing else in your Dropbox. There is no code to paste.

Connect (once on each device)

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the app and tap 📦 Dropbox Sync (next to ☁ Drive Sync).
  2. Tap 🔗 Connect Dropbox. The Dropbox page opens: sign in and tap Allow.
  3. You come back to the app and the panel shows "✓ Dropbox connected" and your Dropbox e-mail.
If you tap ⬆ Save or ⬇ Load before connecting, the app connects first and then does what you asked.

Save and load

  1. Tap ⬆ Save to Dropbox to save this device's data. The status line shows "✓ Saved to Dropbox" and the time.
  2. On the other device, connect Dropbox with the same account, then tap ⬇ Load from Dropbox. Confirm: this replaces that device's data with the Dropbox copy, then the app reloads.
Last save wins, as with Drive. Always Load on a device before you start working on it, and Save when you finish. If the Dropbox copy is newer than this device's last sync, the app warns you before overwriting it.
Auto-sync (this device): tick it and the app checks Dropbox for a newer copy when you open it (it asks before loading) and saves your changes a few seconds after you edit. Conflicts are never overwritten automatically.

Drive or Dropbox?

  • Use one of the two. Both work, but if you save on Drive from one device and on Dropbox from another, the two copies drift apart.
  • The file has the same format, so you can move from one to the other: Load from the old one, then Save to the new one.
  • Disconnect (in the panel) removes the link on this device only. The file in your Dropbox stays. You can also remove access from your Dropbox account settings (Connected apps).
