XKDG App — Directions & Travel

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

This guide covers the tools for moving in a favourable direction:

  1. The 🧭 DIRECTIONS button and its menu (Purpose, 7-day scan).
  2. Air travel: the Direction Calculator (direction and palace of a destination, flight dates).
  3. The Travel Planner: a car trip from A to B with favourable legs, cash stops, charging and Google Maps.
  4. The Live compass: your direction from an origin, live, while you drive.
  5. Lucky Trip: day trips, themed multi-day trips, city tours and events.
Three rules behind every result.
  • Directions are read on the Rotating (转盘) Qimen chart, never on the flying chart.
  • Directions are magnetic (compass), not map north. The app corrects for the local declination.
  • The double-hours are computed in local true solar time. The app converts them to your phone's clock for you. Never add or remove an hour yourself.

Before you start

  • Load the traveller on the main page (Person A, and Person B if you travel together), with birth date and time. Without a person the planner can only give the direction score, not the hour score.
  • Allow location (GPS) for the app. The origin, the compass and "replan from here" all use it.
  • The sections marked 🔒 (Travel Planner, Lucky Trip, Divinations, Birth charts) ask for an access code the first time. Type the code you received and tap Unlock. Air travel is free.

Part 1 — The 🧭 DIRECTIONS button

  1. On the main page tap 🧭 DIRECTIONS (the blue button in the top bar). The 🧭 Directions menu opens.
  2. At the top, choose the 🎯 PURPOSE (applies to all sections below): — Any —, Health, Career, Wealth, Relationship, Journey, Speak or Legal.
  3. Tap 🔎 Scan next 7 days to see, without any destination, the date · weekday · double-hour and the direction that are favourable for that purpose. Each row also shows the stems, door, spirit and star of that palace. A row with ★ on a yellow background has the Wu 戊 / Bing 丙 pair in that palace (Green Dragon Returns / Bird Falls into the Cave); these rows go to the top.
  4. Below are the sections: ✈️ Air travel, 🚗 Travel Planner 🔒, 🍀 Lucky Trip 🔒, 🔮 Divinations 🔒, 🎴 Birth charts 🔒.

Part 2 — Air travel: the Direction Calculator

Use it to know in which direction and palace a destination lies, and to find good flight dates.

1. Origin and destination

  1. In the Directions menu tap ✈️ Air travel. The 🧭 Direction Calculator opens.
  2. 📍 ORIGIN (your location) is already filled with your current position. To change it, type a city in Address or City and tap 🔍 Find, or tap 📡 GPS.
  3. 🎯 DESTINATION: type the city (e.g. Milano, Italy) and tap 🔍 Find. The line under it says "✓ Found: …".
  4. Optional: choose the 🎯 PURPOSE for this direction. It is the same Purpose used by the BEST/LIST scans on the main page.

The app remembers origin and destination for the next time.

2. Calculate the direction

  1. Tap 🧭 CALCULATE DIRECTION.
  2. You see an arrow, Direction: … (Palace …) and the Magnetic bearing in degrees (with the true bearing and the local declination next to it).
  3. The green box says Direction filter active!: from now on the Purpose scans on the main page show only hours with a matching Qimen configuration towards that direction. On the main page a small label 🧭 →NE (Palace 8) reminds you the filter is on.
  4. To remove it, tap ✕ Clear Direction Filter.

3. Scan flight dates (one tap)

  1. With origin and destination set, tap 🔭 SCAN flight dates.
  2. The app calculates the direction, sets Purpose to Journey, closes the panel and scans the current month. The calendar shows ✈ marks on the favourable hours. Move to the next months to scan them too.
A person must be loaded (Person A/B) to score flight dates. If not, the app says "Load Person A/B … first".

Part 3 — The Travel Planner (by car)

The planner builds the whole trip: during each favourable double-hour you drive in the favourable direction, you stop to "cash" it, then you continue towards the destination.

1. Open it and fill the trip

  1. In the Directions menu tap 🚗 Travel Planner. The 🚗 Travel Direction Planner opens (a short ❔ Guide appears the first time; tap Got it).
  2. 📍 Origin and 📍 Destination: type any place and tap 🔍 Find, or tap 📍 GPS. 🕘 lists your recent places. The default trip is Vienna → Rome, so always check these two fields.
  3. Departure (date) and Departure (time).
  4. Trip duration (hours) (leave 12: the app shortens it to the real driving time + about 2 h) and Max drive hours per leg (default 2.5).
  5. UTC offset (base) and Daylight saving (DST) - auto from date are set automatically. Check them only if you start in another time zone.
  6. Stops: leave Automatic (planner chooses). Choose My charging stops (I enter them) only if you already know your charging times (see section 5).
  7. Leave Worker URL (route proxy) as it is: it gives the real road.
📁 Itinerary (at the top): type a name (e.g. Home → Tuoro) and tap 💾 Save to keep a trip you make often. Next time pick it and tap 📂 Load, then SCAN TRIP. 🗑 deletes it.

2. Run the plan

  1. Tap SCAN TRIP. The app scans the trip dates for the loaded person, fetches the real road and builds the plan.
  2. At the top you read 🧭 Bearing to [destination]: …° (≈ NE), then a green line 📍 Directions follow the real road (or an orange one Using a straight line if no road was found).
  3. If you see ⚠️ Hour score unavailable, load the person and tap SCAN TRIP again.

3. Read the plan

The green box 🗺️ Suggested plan — N stops (≥20 min) lists the trip in order:

  • 🚗 Drive 10:20→12:05 (1.8h) toward NE: drive in this time towards that direction. →Rome means "towards the destination".
  • 🛑 Stop ≥20 min at 12:05: a cash stop (see Part 4), then set off toward ….
  • 🔌 Charge 30 min at …: a charging stop, then the time and direction to set off again.
  • 🏁 arrive at [destination] on the last leg.

Tap any line (🔍) to open the Leg detail: the Rotating Qimen chart of that hour with the chosen palace highlighted, and the XKDG setting (from LIST) for the person.

Lower down, Detail by double-hour shows each double-hour with its clock time, its TST (true solar time) and the 8 directions as chips: green = usable, with the combined score; an orange ring = towards the destination. hour ✓ 9 (+8) means the hour is good for the person.

How scoring works (from the app's guide): a direction must pass the Qimen gate (San Qi + a favourable door) to be usable. The hour adds a bonus when its score is ≥ 8: strong at departure and arrival (+8), lighter on the way (+3).

4. Choose a better time

  • 🕐 Departure options (BEST / BY HOUR) ranks the departure times of the chosen day. BEST sorts by whole-trip score, BY HOUR by time; ⭐ marks the best. Each row shows the arrival, the score and how many hours are fortunate. Tap a row (Plan →) to build that itinerary.
  • 🎯 Arrive by (flexible departure): type the Arrival time and a Tolerance (min), then tap FIND ARRIVE-BY SOLUTIONS. You get up to 5 solutions, shortest first (Leave … → arrive …, favourable directions, 🔌 charge needed). Tap Use to plan one. Run SCAN TRIP first so the real driving time is used.
  • On the road, if something changes, tap 🔄 I'm here now — reset origin to my GPS & replan.

5. Electric car (optional)

The first time you open the Travel Planner it asks what car you drive: ⚡ Electric or ⛽ Petrol / Diesel / Hybrid. With a petrol, diesel or hybrid car there are no range or charging fields and no charging stops. You can change it on the Car: line of the planner (change).

  1. In 🔋 Range & charging type Current charge (%) and Full range @100% (km): Remaining range (km) fills itself. Set Safety reserve (%) (default 15).
  2. Tick the charging networks you use; ⭐ Preferred networks only excludes the others.
  3. An Open Charge Map API key must be saved once (paste it, 💾 Save). It stays on this device only.
  4. After SCAN TRIP, 🔌 Find charging stops lists fast chargers (≥150 kW, or 80 kW if nothing else) that you can reach within your range. They can be added to the Maps route.
  5. If you charge at times you choose, set Stops to My charging stops and type them, e.g. 15:30×45, 19:30×30. Each charge is a reset; the planner may suggest charging one double-hour earlier or later.

6. Open in Google Maps

  1. In the box 🗺️ Send to Google Maps, tick the stops to include (🔍 Maps shows one point). 🚩 Exit NE quadrant · 12:05 · near … is a cash stop.
  2. Optional: Add your own stops, separated by ";".
  3. Tap 📍 Open in Google Maps, or 🔗 Copy link.
Maps does not open by itself. Leave 🚗 Auto-open Maps off unless you want an AI-planned trip to jump to Maps automatically.

Part 4 — Cash stops, detours and the arrival

  • Fortunate window: a double-hour in which one direction passes the Qimen gate (and, for the person, the XKDG hour can add its bonus).
  • Cash stop: during a fortunate window you drive in its direction. Where your net direction from the start would leave that 45° quadrant, the planner puts a stop of at least 20 minutes. Stopping there "cashes" the window. Then you set off towards the destination.
  • Detour: if the direct road gives few good legs, the planner tries a real detour. When it is worth it you see "↪ positive detour adopted via … — +12% road time total (within budget)". The detours are limited, so the trip never becomes much longer.
  • Arrival rule: arriving at the destination inside a fortunate window whose direction matches the overall origin → destination direction is a cash in itself, worth more than a road-side stop. No stop is needed: arriving in that window is enough.
The best itinerary is the most favourable one with the shortest practical travel time. A small gain in luck is not worth many extra hours.

The itinerary card in the chat

When a trip is planned through the assistant, a card appears in the chat: origin → destination · direction and degrees · real road km · driving time, then one line per double-hour: CASH toward … (stop there), DETOUR toward …, or DRIVE toward … · no fortunate window (no cash), with door and score. It has a 📍 Open in Google Maps button and a 🔁 Replan from here button.

Part 5 — The Live compass

The round blue 🧭 button at the bottom-left opens the 🧭 Live compass. It works on its own, with or without a trip.

1. Start it

  1. Tap 🧭 (bottom-left) and allow the location.
  2. Open ⚙ Origin / Destination and choose:
    • 📍 Here — use my current position as origin, or
    • From a place… (e.g. Arezzo) for a place you have already left behind;
    • optional 🎯 To a place… (or a direction such as NW).
  3. Tap ▶ Go.

2. Two modes

  • No destination = live mode. Big numbers show your direction from the origin (degrees and quadrant, e.g. 52° NE), the distance, your Heading and speed, and the countdown to the next double-hour. 🚩 Exit NE → E in 4.2 km predicts where you will leave the current quadrant (🔍 Maps shows the point).
  • With a destination = constant course. It shows the course A → B in degrees and the distance, and tells you ✓ On course or Steer RIGHT / LEFT.

During a planned trip it also shows Favourable now: NE and warns "⚠ About to leave NE … consider stopping to cash it" or "⚠ You have LEFT the NE quadrant" (also by voice).

3. Controls (top bar)

Auto / Origin (trip reference) · the octant rose (tap the map to probe any point) · 🔊 / 🔇 voice alerts · ↻ refresh · ⛶ full screen · × close. ⛶ Drive view — full-screen map enlarges the map. The screen stays on while the compass is open.

While driving, you can just tell the assistant: "enlarge the map", "shrink", "recalculate", "recenter", "reset origin", "close the compass".

Part 6 — Lucky Trip (no fixed destination)

  1. In the Directions menu tap 🍀 Lucky Trip.
  2. Choose a tab:
    • Explore an area: several real places around an area, each reachable in a propitious direction and hour. You choose.
    • Themed trip: a multi-day itinerary from your Base town (where you sleep), with Days and an optional Area to explore (stay within).
    • City tour: one day of famous places inside a city, each visited in the hour when its direction from the base is favourable.
    • 🎟 Events: real dated events near your base (next 30 days, about 80 km) whose direction is favourable on the event's own date.
  3. Fill the place (or 📍 GPS), the Date, the Minimum distance from base and tick one or more Categories (e.g. Sacred nature, Spiritual / sacred, Healing / wellbeing, Deep culture, Popular classics…).
  4. Optional: 🌿 Off the beaten path prefers quiet places, without ever overriding the favourable direction.
  5. Tap 🍀 Find lucky trips. The proposals appear in the AI chat.

Part 7 — Divinations and Birth charts

Both draw the Rotating Qimen chart of an hour. Set Date and Time (or Now), tap Draw chart. In Birth charts you can take the date from Person A / Person B. Write your Strategy / notes, tap 💾 Save with a name (it goes in the Archive), or 💬 Send strategy to assistant. If the longitude is missing the chart is not drawn: true solar time needs it.

Part 8 — How the purpose and the person change the result

  • Purpose: with a purpose, a direction must also show that purpose's door (e.g. Wealth → Birth door 生, Career → Open 開 or View 景, Health / Relationship / Journey → Rest 休, Speak → View 景, Legal → Shocking 驚). — Any — accepts any favourable door. The purpose filters the 🔎 Scan next 7 days, the Air travel direction filter and the assistant's purpose searches.
  • Person: the loaded person gives the hour score (XKDG). A good hour (≥ 8) adds a bonus to the direction, strongest at departure and arrival. Without a person you see hour n/a and only the direction score.

Part 9 — Let the assistant do it

Many trip functions are easiest through the assistant (💬). It opens the planner, fills it and runs it for you. Name the places; you do not need coordinates.

Examples you can write:
  • "Plan my trip from Tuoro to Vienna tomorrow" — it picks the most favourable departure of the day and posts the itinerary card.
  • "Find me the best day to drive to Florence in the next week" — a ranked list; tap Choose to open the full plan.
  • "I need to be in Perugia by 18:00" — arrive-by solutions, shortest first.
  • "Where could I go today that is most fortunate, up to 200 km?" — several lucky round trips (out, stay, back).
  • "Next positive day with the day chart to go towards Rome" — positive days on the Qimen day chart, then the good hours of each day.
  • "When is a good business trip towards NW in the next days?" — purpose Career on the travel direction.
  • On the road: "Replan from here" or "Start the compass from here".
For an electric car, tell it the range (e.g. "280 km of range left"): it then finds the charging stops itself.
