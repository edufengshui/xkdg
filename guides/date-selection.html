XKDG App — Date Selection

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

This guide covers the date scanner: the panel below the person charts where you search a period for good dates and hours. It has four ways to show the results:

  1. 📅 BEST: the best hours of the whole period, best first.
  2. ☰ LIST: day by day, the good hours of each day.
  3. 🗓 CAL: a month calendar, coloured by how good each day is.
  4. 📋 TABLES: every hour of every day, with the hexagrams drawn.

The other buttons in the same row (🧭 FENG SHUI, 📊 CHARTS, 🧭 DIRECTIONS) open other tools and are not covered here.

Before you start

  • Set your location. At the top of the app, tap GPS (or check UTC and LONG). Every hour the scanner shows is a Chinese double-hour in true solar time at that position, and each day changes at true-solar midnight.
  • Load the person in ☯ PERSON A (and ☯ PERSON B if the date is for two people): birth date and hour, then CALCULATE. If you only know the birth year, set PILLARS to 1 — Year only and choose the YEAR PILLAR.
  • Without a person the scanner still works, but it gives general dates, not dates for that person. A PURPOSE always needs a person.
Switching a person off. Tap the ☯ PERSON A or ☯ PERSON B header to close that panel: the scanner then ignores that person. Tap it again to bring the person back.

Part 1 — Run a scan

1. Choose the view

Tap 📅 BEST, ☰ LIST, 🗓 CAL or 📋 TABLES. The active one turns blue. The app remembers your choice next time.

2. Choose the purpose (optional)

  1. In PURPOSE, choose one: 🏥 Health, 💼 Career, 💰 Wealth, ❤️ Relationship, ✈️ Journey, 🎤 Speak, ⚖️ Legal. Leave — Any — for a general search.
  2. Tap 📖 Guide next to it to read what this purpose looks for: 1 · A good date (XKDG) and 2 · Feng Shui activation (QMDJ), plus the general rules. Tap ✕ or outside the card to close it.

With a purpose, the scanner keeps only the hours that meet that purpose's structure.

If no person is loaded, the app puts PURPOSE back to — Any — and asks you to input Person A or B first.

3. Two people: the ⚖ button

When both Person A and Person B are loaded, a button appears next to PURPOSE:

  • ⚖ A Priority (default): the score is calculated for Person A.
  • ⚖ Balanced: the score is the average of A and B.

Tap it to switch, then scan again. In both cases the date must connect with both people.

4. Set the period

  1. FROM: the first day of the search. Tap NOW to start from today.
  2. DAYS: how many days to search (30 by default). Type a number, or use the steppers:
    • 1m: − / + removes or adds 30 days;
    • 3m: removes or adds 90 days;
    • 6m: removes or adds 180 days.
Long periods take longer, especially in BEST and TABLES. Start with a month or three, then widen if you need more choice.
If your licence covers a limited date range, days outside it are skipped by the scan.

5. Tap SCAN

Tap the green SCAN button. The results appear under the panel, in the view you chose. If nothing is found, the app says so: remove some filter chips or extend DAYS.

6. Narrow the results with ⚙ FILTER (optional)

  1. Tap ⚙ FILTER. A row of chips opens: Show only (select to filter).
  2. Tap one or more chips to keep only the hours that have them: Adding Elements, Adding Periods, Pure Adding, Hetu Elements, Hetu Periods, Pure Hetu, Pure Qi Elements, Pure Qi Periods, Family Blood Link, Inverse Hex, NaYin, Ke.
  3. Tap a chip again to remove it. If results are already on screen, they update by themselves.
  4. ⚠ Negatives is different: it shows only the negative hours, worst first, in red with a minus sign. Use it to see which hours to avoid.
With Ke the results are sorted by the Ke count; with NaYin by Nayin strength (Power, then plain, then Weak). When a chip is on, a button above the BEST results (⇅ Ke Sort, ⇅ NaYin Sort or ⇅ Chip Sort) switches to ⇅ Score Sort and back.

Part 2 — Read the results

How hours and times are shown

  • Each hour is shown by its name (Zi, Chou, Yin … Hai) and its time range.
  • When your clock time differs from true solar time, the app shows the clock time first and the true-solar range in brackets, marked ✦. Example: 10:10-12:10 (TST 09:00-11:00) ✦.
  • The Zi hour is split at true-solar midnight. In LIST and TABLES, its second half (00:00-01:00) opens the day and its first half (23:00-00:00) closes the same day.
Tap any small badge or tag (a relation, a spirit, Nayin, Ke, N, L, TY…) to see a short explanation of it.

📅 BEST

  1. The best hours of the whole period, highest score first. With a purpose, a blue header shows it, e.g. 💰 Wealth Selection.
  2. Only hours with a score of +6 or more are shown. The greener the row, the closer it is to the top score of this scan.
  3. Each row shows:
    • the score, with A (green) and/or B (purple) for the person it works for;
    • the date, the hour name and its time;
    • the tags: the purpose condition met (e.g. Parent+TY, Parent+Noble, Child+Parent with +n✦ for wealth points, Child+Parent/Adding, Journey, 🎤 Speak), the XKDG relations, the hour spirit (blue = auspicious, red = inauspicious), Nayin and Ke;
    • with a purpose, Qimen badges: 🌀⭐ (activate a stimulator at a palace) and → (act towards a direction). Tap them to open the Qimen chart.
  4. Tap 📅 BY DATE (in the panel) to put the same results in date order. Tap SCAN again to return to best first.

☰ LIST

  1. One block per day. The pink header shows the day, month and year pillars, the date, any solar term (⟐ with its time) and clash marks (⚡).
  2. Under it, the good hours of that day. Each row shows the score (with the purpose icon when it meets the purpose), the time and hour pillar, A/B, the personal stars (N Noble, L Lu, HV, BV, MV, TY), the Qi (red) and Period (blue) numbers of hour, day, month and year, and the relations on the right.
  3. Buttons above the list:
    • ✓ Positive Only / 👁 ALL Hours: hide or show the weak and negative hours;
    • ⇅ Chronological / ⇅ Best First: order of the hours inside each day.

🗓 CAL

  1. Whole months, from the month of FROM to the month where DAYS ends. Days outside the period are faded. Today has a blue border.
  2. The colour of each day comes from its average hour score:
    • yellow: a Family blood-link day;
    • green (darker = better): a good day;
    • white: neutral;
    • red (darker = worse): a negative day.
  3. Marks in the cell: ⚡⚡⚡ day–year branch clash, ⚡⚡ day–month stem clash, ⚡ day–month branch clash; the person's stars (N, L, HV, BV, MV, TY); solar terms (节 blue, 气 orange) with their time.
  4. Move through the months with −6m −2m −1m +1m +2m +6m.
  5. Tap a day to open it in LIST with all its hours. Tap ← Back to Calendar to return.

📋 TABLES

  1. Every day of the period, with every hour, whatever its score.
  2. Each day has a blue band with the date (yellow for today; ⚡ if the day clashes) and three columns: HOUR, DAY · MONTH · YEAR and RELATIONS, with the hexagrams drawn.
  3. Greener rows are higher scores. A coloured banner row marks the hour where a solar term begins.
Use TABLES when you want to check one particular hour, even a bad one. BEST and LIST hide the weak hours; TABLES never does.

Two more buttons (BEST and LIST)

  • 🔓 Only with XKDG: normally an hour with no hexagram relation can still appear if it has Nayin Power and a high score. Tap it (🔒 Only with XKDG) to keep only hours with a real XKDG relation.
  • 🏠 Show Houses: adds badges for the saved houses of the active person that connect with each date. It needs at least one house saved in 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES. Tap 🏠 Hide Houses to remove them.

Part 3 — Open the detail of a result

  1. Tap a result row (in BEST, LIST or TABLES).
  2. The app loads that date and hour into the main chart at the top of the page and scrolls up. You see its four pillars, hexagrams and the full analysis, with Person A and B.
  3. The time placed in the main chart is the clock time for the middle of that double-hour at your position.
  4. Tap ↩ Back to results (bottom left) to go back to the place in the list where you were.
In CAL, tapping a day first opens its hours in LIST; tap an hour there to see its detail.

What the person data changes

  • No person: general dates. You cannot use a PURPOSE.
  • Person A (or only B): the date must connect with the person's birth year; the score adds the person's stars (Noble, Lu, Heaven Virtue, Tian Yi…); results show A (or B).
  • Person A and B: the date must connect with both; the ⚖ button chooses A Priority or Balanced; each result shows A and/or B.
  • Year only (PILLARS = 1): the connection is checked on the year pillar; stars that need the birth day or month are not used.
  • If the active house has guests saved, each guest's birth year must connect with the date too.
