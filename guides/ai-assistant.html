XKDG App — The AI Assistant

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

The 💬 XKDG Assistant is a chat window inside the app. You ask in your own words, and the assistant runs the app's own calculators for you: date selection, Qi Men Dun Jia, Bazi pillars, Feng Shui water and floor plans, travel directions. It does not invent the results: it reads them from the app.

Before you start

  • Update the app: close it completely and open it again.
  • Load the person (Person A, and Person B if needed) with birth date and time. Many answers are personal: without a person, the assistant can only give general results.
  • For Feng Shui questions, save your house in Feng Shui → House Profiles, with its facing and period. The assistant reads the saved houses by name (e.g. "the Vienna house").
  • An internet connection is needed: the assistant works online.

1. Open the assistant

  1. Tap the round purple button 💬 at the bottom right of the screen.
  2. A window opens with the title 💬 XKDG Assistant.
  3. Type your question in the box "Ask something…" and tap Send (on a computer, the Enter key also sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line).
  4. To close the window, tap ✕, or tap 💬 again.

A small grey line under the messages shows what the assistant is doing (for example "Listening…" or "Conversation saved to archive.").

2. Choose the AREA of your question (important)

At the top of the window there is a drop-down menu 🎯 Choose area…. It has these choices:

  • 🧭 Travel & directions: trips, departures, lucky directions, flights, the live compass.
  • 🏠 Feng Shui: houses, flying stars, water (fountain, aquarium), bed and desk, photos of rooms, floor plans.
  • 📅 Dates & divination: good dates for a purpose, hexagram information, divination charts, calendar export.
  • 🔌 Home devices: smart-home connections used only on the Master's own setup. Students can ignore it.

Why it matters

The assistant carries only the tools and instructions of the area you pick. The answer is therefore faster and much cheaper, and the assistant is not distracted by tools it does not need. When an area is chosen, the menu turns orange.

  • If you have not chosen an area, the assistant asks first: "🎯 Which area is your question about?", with one button per area. Tap one and your question is sent.
  • If your question clearly belongs to another area (for example you ask about a fountain while in Travel), the assistant pulls that area in by itself.
  • When the chat is empty, a small yellow line reminds you which area is loaded.
Pick the area before you ask. It is the single easiest way to get a good, quick answer.

3. The language selector

Next to the area menu there is a small selector with 🌐 (Auto), IT, EN, FR. It is the voice language:

  • It decides which language the 🎤 microphone listens in. Choose the language you will speak.
  • 🌐 Auto uses your phone's language.
  • The written reply always follows the language of your own message: write in English and the reply comes in English.

Using your own API key (optional)

Normally the assistant runs on the shared service. If you prefer to pay for your own use:

  1. Create an account and an API key at console.anthropic.com, and set your own monthly spending limit there.
  2. In the assistant, tap ⚙ and find 🔑 My own Anthropic API key.
  3. Paste the key (it starts with sk-ant-) and tap Save.
  4. The window now says "✓ Using your own key — billed directly by Anthropic to your account." Your key goes directly from your device to Anthropic. To go back to the shared service, empty the box and save.
The Copy all settings text contains your keys. Keep it private.

5. The quick chips under the chat (macros)

The small rounded buttons just above the typing box are macros: a short name that sends a long, ready-made instruction.

  • Tap a chip to send it. Some travel chips first open a small window "🚗 How do you want to plan the trip?" (Single day or Find best day, date, departure time), then Go.
  • Long-press a chip (or hover on a computer) to read what it does, without sending it.
  • You can also type the chip's short name in the box and send it.

Make your own macro

  1. Tap ⚡ in the title bar.
  2. Under Add / update a macro, write a short trigger (e.g. wd), a label (what it does) and the full instruction.
  3. Tap Save macro. The new chip appears under the chat.
  4. Use Edit or Delete on any macro in the list. Some chips may come pre-installed; delete the ones you do not need.

6. Photos and voice

📷 Attach a photo

  1. Tap 📷 next to the typing box and take or choose a photo (for example a room seen from the bed or the door).
  2. A small preview appears: "📷 photo ready — add a question and Send". Tap the red ✕ to remove it.
  3. Write your question and tap Send.

For water placement, the assistant opens the photo in the house's Photo sectors view, where the app draws the sectors on it. (See the guide Photo Sectors & Floor Plan.)

🎤 Speak your message

  1. Check the language selector (IT / EN / FR) matches the language you will speak.
  2. Tap 🎤. It turns 🔴 and the status says "Listening…".
  3. Speak. When you stop, your words are sent automatically.
If you do not see 🎤 or 🚗, your browser does not support voice dictation. Typing still works.

7. What to ask: examples by area

The assistant answers best when the question matches one of the app's calculators. Here are examples.

📅 Dates & divination

  • "Find the best dates next week for signing a contract." (good dates for a purpose, for the loaded person)
  • "How is the Wealth purpose coded in the app?"

  • "Which family does hexagram 7 belong to, and what are its qi and yun?"
  • "Make me a calendar file of the best hours for the next months." (downloads a .ics file)

🏠 Feng Shui

  • "Help me place a fountain for the main door." (starts the water interview, see section 8)

  • "Find good dates to move the bed." / "…to set up my work desk."
  • "Which day is positive in the Qimen day chart to change the water in the South?"
  • "Help me build the floor plan of my house."
  • "I have no compass: help me measure the facing with the sun."

🧭 Travel & directions

  • "Plan a trip from Florence to Rome tomorrow morning."
  • "Find the best day and time in the next 7 days to drive to Milan."
  • "Propose a lucky day trip from here today."
  • "I need to arrive in Bologna by 15:00: when should I leave?"

8. Guided interviews

For some tasks the assistant leads you with short questions, one at a time. Just answer each one. You can say "skip" to a step of the water interview.

Water placement (fountain, aquarium, water basin)

Area Feng Shui. Say: "Help me place a fountain for the bed" (or "for the main door", "for the desk"). The steps:

  1. House: the active house; it asks which one only if unclear.
  2. Facing: what the water is for (door, bed, desk, whole house). It reads the saved facing, and asks for degrees only if missing.
  3. People: it names the people it will use and asks if anyone who lives there is missing.
  4. Positions: 3–4 best positions, each with its degree range and why it is good.
  5. The room: which of those spots you can really use (no window, door or furniture in the way).
  6. Photo check: a photo from the facing point, to verify the exact spot (📷).
  7. When: the dates to activate the water, where time, space and people all communicate, with a check button.

Floor plan from laser measurements

Area Feng Shui. Say: "Help me build the floor plan of my house". It asks the internal wall thickness, then each room's two sizes, one measurement at a time, and shows the drawing every 2–3 rooms. Details are in the guide Photo Sectors & Floor Plan.

Measuring the facing with the sun

Area Feng Shui. Say: "Help me measure the facing with the sun". You will need a card, a protractor and a sunny window. The assistant asks, one by one:

  1. Is the sunny window on the front, the back or a side wall?
  2. Lay the card level on the sill, one straight edge flush against the wall (the frame or the glass).
  3. Stand a vertical object on it (e.g. a book opened in a V) and trace the shadow line.
  4. Note the exact time.
  5. Measure the angle between the shadow line and the card edge.
  6. Looking out from inside, does the shadow lean left or right?

It then asks for 1–2 more readings a few minutes apart, and gives the facing and how it compares with your saved facing.

Do it in the morning or afternoon, when the shadow is long, not at a summer noon.

9. Good practices

  • Clear the conversation (🗑) when you change topic. A long chat about travel confuses a new question about water, and costs more. Save it first (💾) if you want to keep it.
  • Choose the right area before asking.
  • Be specific: name the house, the person, the date range, the purpose.
  • It never computes the pillars (gan-zhi) or the hexagrams by hand. It always asks the app's engines. If a value is missing, it says so instead of guessing.
  • Flying stars come only from your saved house (facing and period). If they are not set, the assistant asks you to set them.
  • Answers are indicative. Check important dates with the 🔍 check button and in the app's own panels.
