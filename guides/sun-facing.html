XKDG App — Finding the House Facing with the Sun

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

The ☀️ Sun facing tool measures the facing of a house without a compass. You use the shadow of the sun at a window. The app knows exactly where the sun is at that moment and works out the direction of the window. This guide covers:

  1. When to use it and what you need.
  2. Taking a reading at a sunny window.
  3. Reading the result and saving it as the house facing.
  4. Asking the AI assistant to guide you step by step.

Part 1 — When to use it, and what you need

When to use it

  • When you cannot trust a compass: metal, steel beams, radiators or electric appliances near you can pull the needle.
  • To check a facing you already have from the compass or from the map.

What you need

  • Sun on a window of the house.
  • A card with a straight edge (stiff paper or cardboard) and a pencil.
  • A vertical object with straight sides: a tube or a bottle.
  • A protractor, to measure an angle on the card.
  • A level (or the phone, used as a level) to check that the card lies flat.
  • The house saved in Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES.
  • Your position: allow location access, or open the app's GPS once.
  • The correct date and time on your device, in the local time zone.
The time is everything. The sun moves all the time, so a wrong time gives a wrong facing. Note the time at the exact moment you trace the shadow.

Part 2 — Take a reading

1. Open the tool

  1. Go to Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES.
  2. On the house card, tap ▸ Open.
  3. In the section 📍 Address & facing, tap its ☰, then ☀️ Sun facing.
  4. A window opens with the title ☀️ Sun facing — house name.
  5. Under the instructions you see "Getting your position…", then Position: … with the coordinates and (GPS now) or (last GPS fix).
If it says "No position: allow location access, or open the app's GPS once.", allow location in your browser, or use the app's GPS once, then open the tool again.

2. At the window

Choose a sunny window. Remember which wall it is on: the façade (front), the back, or a side wall.

  1. Lay the card level on the sill. Push one straight edge flush against the wall plane: against the window frame or the glass.
  2. Stand the tube or bottle on the card. Its shadow has two parallel edges: with the pencil, trace the edge nearest the card edge (the side the shadow leans to). Both edges give the same angle, but the nearest one is the easiest to measure.
  3. At that moment, tap Now in the app. This sets the date and time of the reading.
  4. With the protractor, measure the acute angle α (between 0 and 90°) between the shadow line and the card edge, on the side the shadow leans to.
  5. Stand inside, looking out of the window. Does the shadow lean left or right?
Do not use a splayed reveal. The card edge must follow the real wall plane (frame or glass), not the slanted sides of a deep window opening.

3. Enter the reading

Each reading is one row, marked #1, #2…

  1. Check the date and time box. You can correct it by hand (seconds included), or tap Now.
  2. Type the angle in the box α … ° (steps of 0.5°).
  3. In the menu shadow leans… choose left or right.
  4. The result appears under the row at once.
  5. To remove a row, tap ✕ at the end of it.

4. Repeat

  1. Wait a few minutes. The sun moves and the shadow changes.
  2. Tap ➕ Add a measurement. A new row opens with the current time.
  3. Trace the new shadow, measure α again, choose left or right.
  4. Two or three readings are best. The app averages them.

Part 3 — Read the result and save it

Under each row

Example: Sun 212.4° (altitude 31.0°) → window facing 176.8° true, declination 3.9° → 172.9° magnetic (S)

PartMeaning
Sun … ° (altitude … °)Where the sun was at that time: its direction and its height above the horizon.
window facing … ° trueThe window direction from true (geographic) north.
declination … °The difference between true north and magnetic north at your place and date.
… ° magnetic (N, NE, E…)The window direction from magnetic north, with the 8-direction letter. This is the number to use.
Use the MAGNETIC value. All facings in the XKDG app are magnetic, like the luopan. The "true" value is only an intermediate step.

A warning ⚠ may appear under the row:

WarningWhat to do
The sun is below the horizon at that time…Check the date, the time and the time zone.
The sun is very low…The shadow is long and blurred. Wait until the sun is a little higher.
The sun is high: the shadow is short…Take the reading in the morning or the afternoon instead.
The shadow is almost parallel to the window…The sun hits the window from the side. Measure α very carefully, or wait.
The angle α must be between 0 and 90°…Measure the acute (small) angle.
Say whether the shadow leans LEFT or RIGHT…Choose left or right in the menu.

The yellow summary box

  • Window facing: … ° magnetic. With one reading it adds "add one or two more readings to confirm".
  • With several readings: (average of 3, spread ±0.6°). If the spread is more than 1.5°, it says "⚠ the readings disagree, check them".
  • If the house already has a facing: Saved house facing …°: the nearest wall direction is …°, difference …°. Every wall of a house follows the facing, or the facing ±90° or 180°. So the app compares your window with the nearest of these four directions. A small difference confirms the saved facing.

Save it as the house facing

Two buttons appear in the summary box. Choose the one that matches the wall your window is on:

  1. Window on the façade → house facing …°: the window is on the front. The house facing is the window facing.
  2. Window on the back → house facing …°: the window is on the back. The house facing is the window facing + 180°.
  3. The app asks "Save …° as the house facing of "…"?". Tap OK.
  4. It confirms "House facing saved: …°". The house card shows the new Facing …°.
Window on a side wall? The house facing is the window facing +90° or −90°, depending on the side. The app does not do this for you. Work it out, then tap the Facing …° · Period … ✎ text on the house card and type it. Or tell the assistant which side the window is on.
If all floors share the same facing, the saved value applies to the whole house. Otherwise it goes to the floor shown on the card (🏢 Floor:).
The Sun facing window does not show the mountain or the hexagram. Tap 📂 Load on the house card to put the saved facing on the luopan.

Tips for accuracy

  • Morning or afternoon is better than a summer noon: the sun is lower and the shadow is longer.
  • Avoid a sun that is very low on the horizon: the shadow is blurred.
  • The card must lie level, and its edge must be flush with a flat wall plane.
  • Tap Now at the moment you trace the shadow, not later.
  • Take 2–3 readings a few minutes apart. A small spread means a good result.

Another way: 📍 Measure facing (satellite map)

In the same ☰ menu of 📍 Address & facing there is 📍 Measure facing (map). It opens a satellite map of the house address.

  1. Tap a building edge on the facing wall, or tap two points along the wall.
  2. An arrow shows the facing, perpendicular to the wall, pointing out. If it points inward, tap ↔ Flip outward side.
  3. The bar shows Wall, Facing (true), Declination, Facing (magnetic) and the mountain (for example 午 (S)).
  4. Tap ✓ Use this facing. The magnetic value goes into House Facing (°) in the ⭐ FLYING STARS box.
The map and the sun are two independent methods. If both give nearly the same magnetic facing, you can trust it.

Part 4 — Let the AI assistant guide you

The assistant can lead you through the whole measurement, one instruction at a time.

  1. Tap 💬 at the bottom right.
  2. In 🎯 Choose area… pick 🏠 Feng Shui.
  3. Type, for example: "help me measure the facing with the sun". You can name the house, for example "…for the Vienna house".
What the assistant asks, in order:
  1. A sunny window: is it on the façade, the back or a side wall?
  2. Lay a card level on the sill, one straight edge flush against the wall plane (the frame or the glass, not a splayed reveal).
  3. Stand a tube or a bottle on it and trace the shadow edge nearest the card edge.
  4. Note the time at that exact moment.
  5. Measure the acute angle between the shadow line and the card edge with a protractor.
  6. Standing inside looking out: does the shadow lean left or right?
Then it calculates, asks for 1–2 more readings a few minutes apart, and gives you the magnetic facing, the spread and the comparison with the saved house facing.
The assistant saves the facing into the house only after you confirm which wall the window is on. For a side wall it asks which side and adds or subtracts 90°.
