XKDG App — The Daily Email

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

Early every morning the XKDG app can send you an email with your positive hours for today and the next 7 days, computed with the same scanner you use in the app. If you have saved your houses, the email also gives the Feng Shui dates for your fountains, aquariums, bed and desk.

This guide covers:

  1. What the email contains, section by section.
  2. How to request it from inside the app.

Part 1 — What the email contains

The subject is "XKDG · Positive hours · (first day) → (last day)". The email opens with the title Positive hours today and the next 7 days, your name and your city. The sections follow in this order.

1. 📅 Qimen day chart

  • The heading shows the day pillar, Yang or Yin and the Jú number.
  • Two 3×3 charts of the day, South on top like in the app: Rotating · directions and Flying · Feng Shui. Each palace shows the spirit, the heaven and earth stems, the star and the door.
  • Under each chart: the positive palaces of the day, with the hours that are positive in the same palace (clock time, hour pillar, door). If no hour falls there it says "no hour".

2. ⭐ Best of the week

The three best hours of the 8 days, each with its day, time, branch, score and the purposes it is good for.

3. Every day, one after the other

Today's heading is in red; the next 7 days follow, each with its day pillar. For each positive hour you see:

  • the time (from–to) and the branch;
  • 🌙 when it is a night hour (between 23:00 and 05:00);
  • the score: in bold green when it is 18 or more;
  • the purposes: 🩺 Health · 🏆 Career · 💰 Wealth · ❤️ Relationship · ✈️ Journey · 🗣️ Speak · ⚖️ Legal, and the Nayin when it applies.

A day with nothing good says "No positive hour".

4. 🏠 Feng Shui (inside each day)

If your houses are included, each day has a green 🏠 Feng Shui box, one house after the other. Each row shows the time, the branch, the grade and the place:

  • 🐠 Aquarium: graded by tier, T1 to T4. Only the best ones of the day are shown (tier 3–4 first; otherwise tier 2; otherwise tier 1).
  • 💧 Fountain: for a door, the bed or the desk, with the best hour of the day (or "all day").
  • 🛏️ Bed and 🪑 Desk: the dates that suit the placement.
  • ⭐ marks the strongest rows (tier 3–4, a score of 18 or more, or a Full Blood Link).
A house in another time zone is computed at its own place; its rows say (local time).

5. Detail of every hour (at the end)

The top of the email is for a quick look. The why is at the bottom, under Detail of every hour: Hour with you, Day with you, Quality, Hour spirit, Noble / Tian Yi, Nayin, and the Purposes with their scores (and the Qimen reasons). After it, 🏠 Feng Shui detail explains each Feng Shui row: for an aquarium the tier, whether it connects with you, and Why; for a fountain Door with the day and Water with the day; for the bed and desk how each person relates to the placement and to the day.

Time: everything is computed in true solar time of your city (each house at its own place), and daylight saving is handled day by day. The clock times in the email are the ones on your watch.

Part 2 — How to request the email

1. Save yourself as a person

  1. Open ☯ PERSON A.
  2. Fill in NAME, BIRTH DATE and HOUR (set DST: OFF / ON as at your birth).
  3. Tap SAVE.
If you only know your year pillar, choose PILLARS → 1 — Year only and pick it in YEAR PILLAR. The email will then use the year pillar.

2. Save your houses (optional)

Do this only if you want the Feng Shui dates. In Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES save your house (+ Add a new house) with what you want followed:

  • the doors with their facing and the degree of their fountain;
  • the bed (sitting) and the desk (facing), with their fountain if you have one;
  • the aquariums / waters placed in a palace.

The email includes every house where you are the owner, the guest or a member.

3. Build your line

  1. Load yourself in ☯ PERSON A, then go to Feng Shui → 🏠 HOUSE PROFILES.
  2. Tap 📧 Daily email. The window 📧 Daily email settings opens.
  3. Person: your saved name (already filled in).
  4. Email: the address where you want to receive it.
  5. City: Vienna, Tuoro, or My location (GPS). With GPS, type your place name in the box that appears (e.g. Kuala Lumpur): it is the name printed in the email.
  6. include Feng Shui dates (houses): leave it ticked for the house dates; untick it if you want only the hours.
  7. Language: English, Italiano or Français.
  8. Tap Build. A line of text appears, and under it a summary such as "1 house(s), 3 placement(s): …".
  9. Tap 📋 Copy. It says "Copied."
The small technical notes in that window are for Master Hess. You can ignore them.
GPS: if it says "No GPS position yet", allow location for the app (or open the compass once), then tap Build again. The time zone is taken from your phone, so build it where you live.

4. Send it to Master Hess

  1. Paste the copied line into a message to Master Hess and send it.
  2. He adds it. From then on the email arrives early every morning.
Changed a house, moved, or want another language? Open 📧 Daily email again, tap Build and 📋 Copy, and send the new line.
Your data: your birth data and house placements are used only to compute your email.
