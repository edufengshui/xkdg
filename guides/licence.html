XKDG App — Licence: PIN and Moving to Another Device

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

The XKDG app is unlocked with the PIN you receive from Master Hess. This guide covers:

  1. Unlocking the app the first time with your PIN.
  2. Working offline, and what happens after a long time without internet.
  3. Unlocking a second device with a transfer code, without typing the PIN again.

Part 1 — Enter your PIN the first time

The first unlock needs the internet. Connect the device before you start.
  1. Open the app. The screen 🔐 XKDG Calculator asks "Enter your access PIN to continue".
  2. Type your PIN in the box Enter PIN.
  3. Tap UNLOCK (or press Enter). The button says Checking... for a moment.
  4. The app opens. You do not need to type the PIN again on this device.

If you see a message

No connection. The first unlock needs the internet…Connect to the internet and tap UNLOCK again.
Too many wrong attempts. Please wait about ten minutes…Wait about ten minutes, then try again.
No PIN yet? The screen says "Contact your instructor for access": ask Master Hess.

Part 2 — Working offline

  • Once unlocked, the app works without internet, for example on a plane.
  • When you are online, the app quietly checks your licence once a day. You see nothing.
  • Without internet, the app keeps working for 30 days after the last successful check.
  • After 30 days offline, the PIN screen appears with "Checking your licence…". Just connect to the internet and reload the app: it unlocks again by itself. You do not need to type the PIN.
If it says "No connection. Your licence needs a quick online check — connect to the internet and reload.", do exactly that.
If it says "This licence code has been withdrawn. Please enter your PIN.", your old code is no longer valid. Ask Master Hess for your current PIN.

Entering the PIN again (🔑 licence code)

Use this only if Master Hess gives you a new PIN.

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the page, under the version number.
  2. Tap 🔑 licence code.
  3. Confirm "Enter your licence code again?". Nothing else is deleted: your house profiles, settings and saved data stay untouched.
  4. The PIN screen opens. Type the new PIN and tap UNLOCK.

Part 3 — Move the licence to another device

To unlock a second device (a new phone, a tablet, a computer) you can use a transfer code instead of the PIN.

1. On the device that is already unlocked

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
  2. Tap 📤 transfer licence.
  3. The app makes a code that starts with XKDG-. It copies it (if your browser allows it) and opens the share menu, or shows the code in a box.
  4. Send the code to yourself on the other device (message, email, notes), or copy it.
Keep the code private. It unlocks the app like your PIN. Do not share it with anyone else.
If it says "This device has no confirmed licence to transfer", enter your PIN once on this device first, then try again.

2. On the new device

  1. Connect to the internet. The transfer needs the internet.
  2. Open the app. On the PIN screen, tap Have a transfer code from another device?
  3. Paste the whole code (it starts with XKDG-) into the box.
  4. Tap USE TRANSFER CODE. After Checking... the app opens.
