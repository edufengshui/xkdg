XKDG App — The CHARTS tab

A step-by-step guide · Feng Shui Research Center · Master Eduardo Hess

Every chart in the app is in one place: the 📊 CHARTS button, next to 🧭 FENG SHUI in the top bar. Tap it and a list opens, in two groups:

  1. Any date · follows the main calendar: the QMDJ hour chart, the Da Liu Ren chart, the QMDJ Day chart and the QMDJ Month chart. They open on the date (and time) of the main calendar.
  2. On the luopan · current house: QMDJ Li Qi, Sun & Moon formula, Flying star chart, QMDJ annual chart, its ring, and the DLR annual chart. They work on the house you are looking at in Feng Shui. The app switches to FENG SHUI by itself when you pick one of these.

To close the list without choosing, tap ✕ or tap outside it.

Before you start

  • Update the app: close it completely and open it again.
  • Set your location (longitude and UTC offset in the main panel, or GPS). The QMDJ charts use them to work in local true solar time.
  • For the luopan charts, load your house: go to Feng Shui → House Profiles and tap 📂 Load on its card. The house needs its facing (degrees) and its period.
  • For QMDJ Li Qi on a house, save the palace of the external door (see Part 5).
Rotating or Flying? The app follows one rule everywhere: Rotating 轉盤 = human directional actions and divination. Flying 飛盤 = Feng Shui stimulators. In every chart South is at the top.
Time. The Jú, the solar term and the Chinese hour are always decided in local TRUE SOLAR TIME at the hour's own moment: never civil clock time, never Beijing time.

Part 1 — 🎲 QMDJ chart (any date and hour)

The hour chart for any moment. It opens on the date and time of the main calendar, in Rotating, already drawn. If you move the main calendar, then open the chart again, it follows.

1. Draw a chart

  1. Tap 📊 CHARTS → 🎲 QMDJ chart.
  2. In Date, choose the day.
  3. In Hour 時辰, choose the Chinese hour. Each entry shows the branch, its clock time and its pillar (e.g. 午時 … · 甲午).
  4. In Chart, choose Flying 飛盤 or Rotating 轉盤.
  5. Tap Show chart.

2. Move one hour at a time

Under the chart there are two arrows, ◀ and ▶, with the label 1 時辰. Each tap moves the chart one Chinese hour back or forward. After the last hour of the day it goes on to the next day (and back to the previous day before the first hour).

3. The Bazi of the moment

Top right, next to the title, you see the four pillars of the chart's moment (時 日 月 年), coloured by element, with the day pillar highlighted. They are the same pillars as the rest of the app: true solar time, year and month changing at the Jie Qi instant.

4. Zhi Shi

The palace that holds the Zhi Shi 值使 shows a small orange label Zhi Shi ℹ. Tap it for a short explanation; tap OK to close. (The Zhi Fu is not marked separately: you see it from the Commander spirit.)

5. Set Jú / day / hour by hand

Use this when you want a specific chart from the tables, without a date.

  1. Tick Set Jú / day / hour by hand.
  2. In Jú 局, choose Yang Jú 1–9 or Yin Jú 1–9.
  3. In Day stem 日干, choose the day group (e.g. 丙/辛 · Bing-Xin).
  4. In Hour 時辰, choose the hour. The list already has the right stems for that day group (Five Rats rule).
  5. The chart is drawn at once, Flying or Rotating, with the heading "set by hand — the date above is ignored".
In this mode the arrows ◀ ▶ move through the hours of the hand-set menu, and the Bazi box is empty (there is no real date). Untick the box to go back to the date.

Part 2 — 🀄 Da Liu Ren chart (any date and hour)

  1. Tap 📊 CHARTS → 🀄 Da Liu Ren chart. It opens on the date and time of the main calendar.
  2. Change Date and Hour 時辰 if you want (each hour is listed with a clock time, e.g. 午 Wu · 11:30).
  3. Tap Draw chart.

From top to bottom the chart shows:

  • The heading: date, hour, the 月將 month general, your longitude, and day or night.
  • The four pillars: Hour, Day, Month, Year, with stem, branch and element.
  • Three Messages 三傳: Initial, Middle, Final, each with spirit, relation and branch, and the Method used.
  • Four Readings 四課: the branch column reads heaven / earth.
  • Plate 天地盤: tianpan above, dipan below; 空 = void.
  • 12 Heaven Generals 十二天將: each general, Yang or Yin, its heaven and earth branch.
  • Gods and Devils: the spirits of each sector. ● green = favourable, ● red = unfavourable, ○ hollow = a green sharing the sector with a red. Heaven Virtue and Month Virtue come from the month branch 月支.

Close it with ✕.

Part 3 — 📆 QMDJ Day chart and 🗓 QMDJ Month chart

Both open in the same window. The chart is the normal QMDJ chart, but the Jú is found with the day / month method, and the day pillar (or the month pillar) takes the place of the hour.

1. Open and switch

  1. Tap 📊 CHARTS → 📆 QMDJ Day chart or 🗓 QMDJ Month chart. It opens on the date of the main calendar.
  2. Change the Date if you want.
  3. Use Day 日 / Month 月 to switch between the two charts.
  4. Use Rotating 轉盤 / Flying 飛盤 to choose the chart. Rotating is the default. Same Jú, same pillar in both.
  5. The arrows ◀ ▶ under the chart move 1 day 日 (Day chart) or 1 month 月 (Month chart).

Above the chart a line tells you the result, e.g. Yang Jú 4 · day 丙午 · month … · year … (Day) or Yin Jú 7 · month … · year … · 5-year block … (Month).

2. How the Jú is found

Open How the Jú is found at the bottom: the app lists every step it took, with dates.

Day chart — Yang Dun from the Winter Solstice, Yin Dun from the Summer Solstice:

  1. At the 8 main seasonal points (冬至 立春 春分 立夏 夏至 立秋 秋分 立冬) the Jú restarts from that point's Upper, Middle or Lower number. Which one is chosen by the cycle of the month pillar: 甲子 / 甲午 decade = Upper, 甲申 / 甲寅 = Middle, 甲戌 / 甲辰 = Lower.
  2. Every decade of days (from a 甲 day) is one Jú: +1 in Yang, −1 in Yin.
  3. At the entry of the other month branches the Jú moves to that branch's cycle (子卯午酉 Upper, 丑辰未戌 Lower) with the Rule of the Six: ±6 per step. Same cycle = no change.
  4. The change applies from the day of the term, in local true solar time.

Month chart — Yin Dun only. One Jú lasts 60 months (5 years), from a 甲 or 己 year. The Jú goes back one number every 5 years (9, 8, 7 … 1) and the 45-year cycle restarts with Yin 9 in 1864, then 1909, 1954, 1999, 2044 …

3. ✨ Day + hour in the same palace (Day chart only)

Under the Day chart the app looks for the hours of that day that are positive in the same palace where the day chart is positive.

  1. For each positive palace of the day chart you see a box: the palace (e.g. Li S) and its day configuration (stems, door, star, spirit).
  2. Inside it, each matching hour is a green button: pillar · clock time · door (e.g. 甲午 · 12:31 · Open). The clock time is the time at the centre of the hour.
  3. Tap an hour: the 🎲 QMDJ chart opens on that date and hour, in the same mode (Rotating or Flying).
"Positive" = no excluding formation, and San Qi (Tien Pan) / Wu / Commander with a favourable door. If a PURPOSE is selected in the top bar, its door is also required, in both charts. Hours are the 12 double-hours of the day in local true solar time, each with its own Jú.

If nothing qualifies you see "No positive palace in the day chart" or "no hour of this day is positive here".

Part 4 — The luopan charts of the current house

These entries act on the luopan in FENG SHUI. Some labels change to show their state (for example · ON). Pick the same entry again to switch it off.

☀️🌙 Sun & Moon formula

  1. Make sure House Facing (°) is filled in.
  2. Tap ☀️🌙 Sun & Moon formula.
  3. A small window opens (you can drag it; ✕ closes it). It shows the ☀️ Sun (facing 向) mountains and the 🌙 Moon (sitting 山) mountains of the house, each with its date range. ★ marks the main mountain, · its two trine partners (三合, 120° apart). Below, Right now shows where the Sun and the Moon are today.
  4. The same mountains are marked on the luopan.

When the Sun or the Moon reaches the facing (or its trines), the facing has a ~15-day auspicious window: celebrations, invitations, important Feng Shui activations, new business, launches. The reading is yours: the app only shows where they are.

▦ Flying star chart

  1. Fill in House Facing (°) and Period (1-9).
  2. Tap ▦ Flying star chart: the luopan is replaced by the flat Flying Stars card.
  3. To go back, the same entry now reads 🧭 Back to Luopan.

✳️ QMDJ annual chart

The QMDJ Feng Shui annual chart of the house, drawn in the luopan area. The app builds it in three steps:

  1. Period → Jú: the period we are in now (9 until 2044) is the Jú. Yang Dun only: Yang Jú 9.
  2. Year → day: the stem of the solar year picks the day group (2026 丙午 → Bing-Xin).
  3. Facing → hour: the facing, reduced to its double mountain branch, is the hour branch; the hour stem follows from the day group (Five Rats rule).

The heading shows all three, e.g. Yang Ju 9 · Day Bing-Xin · Hour 甲午 · facing …° — mountain … → branch …. It is a Rotating chart. Tap the entry again (QMDJ annual chart · ON) to close it.

The annual chart and the Flying star chart use the same space: opening one closes the other.

✳️ QMDJ annual chart · ring

The same annual chart, but its 8 palace boxes are placed around the luopan, each at its direction, with the Flying Stars still visible in the middle. The heading QMDJ annual · Yang Jú … · facing …° is written at the top.

  • Switching the ring on puts the luopan in its compact size (there is no room for the ring otherwise).
  • The ring and the DLR ring cannot be on together: switching one on switches the other off.

🀄 DLR annual chart

The Da Liu Ren Feng Shui annual chart: the 12 annual spirits drawn as a ring around the luopan.

  1. Tap 🀄 DLR annual chart. The label becomes DLR annual chart ON.
  2. A small panel appears on the luopan (drag it by ∷; double-tap ∷ to put it back in the corner):
    • ◀ year ▶: previous / next year.
    • 📋: the table of the 12 sectors with every positive and negative.
    • 🏠 Plan: the DLR chart on the saved floor plan, with the stars still visible.
  3. Each sector shows a dot: ● auspicious, ● inauspicious, ○ Heavenly Doctor with a negative, ⊘ cancelled. The big branch is the tianpan; "over …" is the dipan, the sector of the house it lands on.

How it is cast: the day pillar is the year pillar (a Tai Sui day of the solar year, from Li Chun to Li Chun), the hour branch is the house facing double mountain, and the month general is the one of that day.

The DLR ring switches off the QMDJ annual ring and the Sun & Moon markers, so the wheel does not get crowded.

Part 5 — 🏠 QMDJ Li Qi · Flying Pan

The permanent QMDJ chart of a space (Flying Pan method). It has no date: it comes from the period of construction or move-in and from the house directions.

The rules the app applies

  • Yang Dun only. The period of the house is the Jú (period 8 → Yang Jú 8).
  • House: the sitting double mountain gives the Fu Shou 符首; the palace of the Qi mouth (the door) is the Zhi Fu 值符 palace.
  • Business: the facing double mountain gives the Fu Shou; the facing palace is the Zhi Fu palace.
  • From these the app finds the hour of the chart (it is shown, e.g. → hour 丙申).

1. Save the door palace (houses only)

  1. In Feng Shui → House Profiles, add the door with + External Door, or edit it with ✏.
  2. When asked "Palace where this door IS (seen from the centre of the house — not where it faces)", type N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W or NW (or Kan, Gen…).

The app takes the first external door that has a palace as the Qi mouth.

2. Draw the chart

  1. Load the house (📂 Load in House Profiles).
  2. Tap 📊 CHARTS → 🏠 QMDJ Li Qi · Flying Pan. The top line shows the house name, its facing and its period.
  3. First step, "This space is a:" tap 🏠 House or 🏢 Business.
  4. For a house, check the Qi mouth menu. The first entry is the saved door (🚪 Front door · Li). You can also choose any palace by hand. If no door palace is saved, choose one here.
  5. The line above the chart shows the reasoning: Yang Jú … · Sitting …° = 壬子 → Fu Shou … · Zhi Fu in … → hour ….
  6. To change House / Business, tap change next to the label.
If the sitting (or facing) is within 1.5° of a double-mountain edge, the app warns you: check the chart of the next mountain too.

3. Read the chart

It is the normal Flying chart. Each palace also shows its Di Pan spirit in small italics, as 地 X (for example 地 Bird): the spirit that goes with that palace's earth stem. Read it together with the heaven spirit.

4. ✨ Interpretation

  1. Under the chart, tap ✨ Interpretation.
  2. Wait for "⏳ Interpreting the chart…". The reading appears below: the overall quality and the Qi mouth, then one paragraph per palace (Qi mouth, facing, sitting, then the others), then the occupants by their birth year stem.
AI reading, indicative only: it is not a substitute for a consultation. The AI follows the reading method of the Li Qi chapter. It writes in Italian, French or English, following the 🌐 language chosen in the assistant (or your phone's language).
